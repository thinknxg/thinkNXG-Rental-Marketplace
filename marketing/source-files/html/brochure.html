Challenge & solution

From phone tag to a structured tender.

Sourcing scaffolding, formwork and equipment usually means chasing several suppliers and stitching their answers together. The marketplace turns that into one requirement and one comparable set of offers.

The usual way

Calling yard after yard

Finding who has stock and a price takes call after call.

Quotes that don't compare

Every supplier quotes in a different format.

Stock promised twice

Free stock is checked by phone, then promised twice.

No record of the decision

Clarifications end up scattered across calls and emails.
With the marketplace

One requirement, many suppliers

Post one brief and matched suppliers come to you.

Comparable offers

Every offer is totalled by the same rules, line by line.

No double booking

Accepted quantities are locked for your dates.

Full audit trail

Every revision, clarification and decision is recorded.

Sealed by default

Suppliers never see each other's prices. Tender confidentiality can be set to Sealed, Rank Only or Open, and the customer's identity is disclosed to suppliers at the stage the tender is configured for (on award by default in the product demo).

Tender & bidding process

A tender engine built for fairness.

Every tender follows the same state machine, so every supplier is treated by the same rules.

Published
Supplier Matching
Invitation Sent
Bidding Open
Bidding Closed
Evaluation
Awarded

Confidentiality you control

Sealed, Rank Only or Open offers. Customer identity disclosure on award, on shortlist or always.

Evaluation

Technical acceptance, then commercial acceptance, then award.

Clarification & negotiation

Questions to one or all suppliers; revised offers keep a full history.

Integrity

Accepting an offer locks tender, offer and stock rows together: two customers cannot be booked against the same stock for overlapping dates.
Offer totals are calculated by the marketplace. Product screenshot, demo data.

Same rules for every supplier

The marketplace calculates every total on the server from the supplier's inputs, so figures typed in a browser can never change the price. Hire is priced per day, week or month pro-rata; contracts by month; sales by unit; charges and tax are added in the same way for every offer.

Rental fulfilment

Marketplace to rental, in one journey.

When a booking is confirmed, the marketplace can hand it to thinkNXG Rental, the rental management and fulfilment engine, as a draft Hire Order (or a Sales Order for a sale). Status then flows back to the booking.

1

Hire Order

Commercial order with availability and shortfall per line
2

Rental Contract

Rates, billing cycle, grace days, minimum hire, deposit
3

Material Reservation

Soft reservation per warehouse; shortfall can go to cross hire
4

Delivery Order

Dispatch from yard to site
5

Material at Site

Ledger of what is on each site
6

Off-Hire & Inspection

Partial or full returns, inspection, damage and loss settlement

Multi-location warehouses

Own and cross-hire warehouses at any locations; returns split per row.

Recurring rental billing

Billing schedules for material on hire, with off-hire and final settlement.
The hand-off applies to suppliers who run thinkNXG Rental on the same site. Job-type contracts (Hire Order Contract / JCR) are not yet mapped from marketplace bookings.

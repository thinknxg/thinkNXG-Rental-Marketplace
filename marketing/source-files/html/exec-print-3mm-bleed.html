ThinkNXG Rental Marketplace

Premium Tender/Bid Scaffolding & Equipment Rental Marketplace. Powered by thinkNXG.

Positioning

A premium digital marketplace connecting construction companies, contractors, equipment rental businesses and suppliers. Customers post one requirement; matched suppliers compete with sealed offers; the customer compares, selects, books and follows fulfilment.

Two products, one journey

ThinkNXG Rental Marketplace sources and awards. thinkNXG Rental runs the rental: reservations, delivery, material at site, billing, returns and settlement.

Scope notes

Stripe and Razorpay payment connectors are built and their webhook handling is tested, but need a provider sandbox check before going live; bank-transfer payments confirmed by Finance work today. Supplier payouts are recorded by Finance, not paid automatically. The marketplace-to-rental hand-off applies to suppliers on the same site. The interface is English-only for now.
